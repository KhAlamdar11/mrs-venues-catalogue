# Changelog

Weekly updates, newest first. Written by the `/update-catalogue` run.

## 2026-10-01 — refocused on multi-robot systems
- **Scope change:** the catalogue now targets multi-robot systems research (coordination, MAPF, swarms, distributed control, multi-agent learning). Venues get an `area` — 🐝 dedicated multi-robot & swarm (`mrs`), 🤖 general robotics, 🎛️ control & networked systems, 🧠 AI / planning / learning — which splits the upcoming-deadlines tables. Special issues are now only listed if they are on a multi-robot topic.
- **New conferences:** MRS (IEEE Multi-Robot & Multi-Agent Systems; 2025 Singapore, deadline 9 Jul 2025; 2027 in Granada, dates TBA), ANTS (2026 Darmstadt, deadline 20 Nov 2025), SWARM 2027 (Rayong, 21 Mar 2027, tentative), ICAPS 2027 (14 Dec 2026), SoCS, ALIFE (2027 in Prague), ECC 2027 (31 Oct 2026), NecSys.
- **New journals:** Swarm Intelligence, JAAMAS, npj Robotics, T-CNS, TAC, Automatica, L-CSS.
- **Changed:** AAMAS moved to the multi-robot area; AAMAS 2027 confirmed on the official site (abstract 1 Oct, paper 8 Oct 2026). DARS moved to the multi-robot area and B → A. L4DC, CDC and ACC moved to the control area.
- **New special issues:** npj Robotics "Swarm Robotics" (19 Dec 2026) and "Intelligent Space Robots" (23 Jun 2027, multi-robot track); five Frontiers RAI Multi-Robot Systems research topics (30 Oct 2026 – 1 Jun 2027).
- **Removed (out of scope):** conferences CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, HRI, RO-MAN, Humanoids, RoboSoft, BioRob, AIM, ROBIO; journals T-MECH, THRI, Soft Robotics, T-MRB, IJSR, ASME JMR; special issues T-ASE GenAI (deadline was 1 Oct), T-ASE AI-enabled DES, Frontiers embodied-AI discovery, TMECH/AIM focused section.
- **Not added:** Science Robotics swarm-robotics special issue (deadline 30 Sep 2026 already passed; read from search snippets only); Swarm Intelligence ANTS 2026 special issue (invitation only); Frontiers human–swarm interaction and cooperative-learning topics (extensions only).
- **Suggestions for the maintainer:** tiers of the new venues are a first guess (MRS, ANTS, DARS at A; SWARM, npj Robotics at B). Possible further additions: ECMR (European Conference on Mobile Robots, biennial), IFAC World Congress (triennial), GECCO (evolutionary swarm robotics).
