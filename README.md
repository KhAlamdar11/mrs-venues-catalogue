## Methodology

**Scope.** This catalogue is for multi-robot systems research: multi-robot coordination, task allocation and planning (incl. multi-agent path finding), swarm robotics, distributed estimation and control, multi-agent learning, and multi-UAV / fleet deployments. It tracks the dedicated multi-robot, swarm and multi-agent venues (🐝), the general robotics flagships where most multi-robot papers appear (🤖), and the control (🎛️) and AI / planning / learning (🧠) venues where multi-robot work routinely appears. Sub-field venues with little multi-robot work (soft, medical, humanoid, HRI, vision) are deliberately left out. Special issues are only listed if multi-robot / swarm / multi-agent systems are their theme or an explicit topic.

**Tiers** reflect standing *within the robotics and multi-robot community* (selectivity, citation impact, and how a paper there is perceived by hiring/tenure committees in robotics). Adjacent control and AI venues are tiered by overall prestige. Tiers are opinionated — edit `tier:` in `data/*.yaml` to taste.

**Predictions.** When a venue has not announced its next deadline, the most recent known deadline is shifted forward by the venue's cadence (1 year, or 2 for biennial venues), keeping the same month/day. Most robotics venues are very regular (e.g. ICRA ≈ 15 Sep, IROS ≈ 1 Mar), so this is usually accurate to within ~2 weeks; the smaller multi-robot symposia (MRS, DARS, ANTS, SWARM) move around more. Predicted dates are prefixed with `~` and marked 🔮.

**Updates.** Data is refreshed weekly by Claude using `.claude/commands/update-catalogue.md` (see [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) for what changed and [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for the data schema).
